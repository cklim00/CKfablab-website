This page holds on-site photos from care centres, dementia units and kindergartens. To respect the privacy of the elders and children pictured, it is password-protected. Please enter the password to continue. 本頁收錄枕頭戰於各長照中心、失智據點與幼兒園的實地照片。為尊重被攝長者與孩童的肖像權，設有密碼保護。請輸入密碼後瀏覽。


Pillow Fight travelled to elder day-care centres, dementia-care units, kindergartens and community associations across Taiwan, 2019–2020. 枕頭戰於 2019–2020 年巡迴台灣各地日照中心、失智照護據點、幼兒園與社區發展協會的實地紀錄。

Please keep these images confidential and do not redistribute. 請勿轉載或外流這些照片。
