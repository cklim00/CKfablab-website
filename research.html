Research研究

Design as an
adaptive system 設計，作為一個
適應性系統

Our research asks how design — its tools, processes and education — adapts when computation, AI and fabrication become native design media. Grounded in CAAD and design cognition research, extended into today's generative AI era. 我們探問：當運算、AI 與製造成為原生設計媒材時，設計的工具、流程與教育如何隨之適應。研究根植於 CAAD 與設計認知，延伸至當代生成式 AI。

01 — AI-Augmented Design

AI-Augmented Design & CreativityAI 增強設計與創造力

We study generative AI as a design partner: how designers ideate, prototype and reflect with conversational AI; vibe coding as a new literacy for design students; and generative art as creative practice. Current work examines how non-programmers build working interactive systems through natural-language collaboration with AI. 我們將生成式 AI 視為設計夥伴：設計師如何與對話式 AI 發想、原型與反思；vibe coding 作為設計學生的新素養；生成藝術作為創作實踐。目前研究聚焦非程式背景者如何透過自然語言與 AI 協作建構可運作的互動系統。

02 — Interaction & Gerontechnology

Interaction Design for Intergenerational Care面向代間關懷的互動設計

A decade of designing with and for elders: tangible game devices (Pillow Fight, commercialized 2019 and awarded at the Stanford Design Challenge), community co-creation through USR programs, and AI conversational companions supporting dementia care. Design here means working directly in the field — day-care centers, community sites, real caregivers. 十年與高齡者共同設計的累積：實體遊戲裝置（枕頭戰，2019 商品化並獲史丹佛銀髮設計競賽獎項）、USR 計畫的社區共創，以及支援失智照護的 AI 對話夥伴。這裡的設計意味著直接走入場域—日照中心、社區據點、真實的照顧者。

03 — Digital Fabrication

Digital Fabrication & Maker Education數位構築與 Maker 教育

From freeform architectural construction (AleppoZONE, Setiakon) to parametric fabrication and Taiwan's first maker MOOC with 750+ learners. We treat making as thinking: CAD/CAM, Rhino/Grasshopper and physical computing as media for design reasoning, taught to cross-disciplinary students. 從自由形體建築構築（AleppoZONE、Setiakon）到參數化製造，以及全台第一門、750 餘人修習的 maker MOOC。我們視「做」為「思考」：CAD/CAM、Rhino/Grasshopper 與 physical computing 是設計推理的媒材，教授給跨領域學生。
