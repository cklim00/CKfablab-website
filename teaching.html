Teaching教學

Teaching designers
to make with AI 教設計人
與 AI 一起做

Eighteen years of making technology learnable for design students — from CAD/CAM and physical computing to today's AI-augmented design studios. The through-line: beginners can build real, working things. 十八年來讓科技對設計學生變得可學—從 CAD/CAM、physical computing 到今日的 AI 設計課。一以貫之的信念：初學者也能做出真正可運作的東西。

Teaching Methodology教學方法論

01 — CT + DT

CT + DT interwoven運算思維 × 設計思考無縫交織

A methodology weaving computational thinking with design thinking in five steps — define, ideate, pattern-recognize, build, iterate — recognized as an MOE Teaching Practice Research Outstanding Project (AY109). 自創「CT+DT 無縫交織」教學法，五步驟：需求定義→創意發想→模式辨識→技術實作→反饋改進—獲教育部 109 年教學實踐研究績優計畫肯定。

02 — Human-AI

Seeing-Instructing-SeeingSeeing-Instructing-Seeing AI 設計框架

An iterative framework treating AI as a design collaborator — redefining the designer's role from sole creator to director of AI co-creation. Not replacing designers; expanding their boundaries. 以循環迭代為核心的原創設計框架，將 AI 視為協作夥伴—設計者的角色從創作者轉為 AI 共創的指導者。不是取代設計師，而是擴展設計師的能力邊界。

03 — Design-to-Fabrication

Think it, make it想得到，做得出

A three-stage flipped model — pre-class theory, in-class digital fabrication, project integration — carrying every idea through design to physical delivery. 課前理論、課堂數位構築實作、專案整合應用三階段翻轉教學—強調從設計到製造的一貫流程，讓每個想法都被做出來。

Open Education開放教育

Taiwan's first
maker MOOC全台第一門
maker 磨課師

MOE-funded MOOCs on digital making and interaction design (2014, 2015) brought digital fabrication to the public — 750+ registered learners including 200+ from overseas. On campus, flipped classrooms bring design, art and engineering students together to build interactive works. 2014、2015 連續兩年獲教育部磨課師計畫，將數位構築課程開放給一般民眾—註冊人數逾 750 人，含 200 多位海外學員。校內則以翻轉教室帶領設計、藝術與工程跨領域學生共同完成互動作品。

Also: senior-high outreach courses (Wuling Senior High multi-disciplinary elective; Zhushan Senior High workshops) and the AI Creative Design Thinking general-education course. 另開設高中課程（武陵高中多元選修、竹山高中工作坊）與「AI 創意設計思考」通識課。

small hands hold old hands大手牽小手，
