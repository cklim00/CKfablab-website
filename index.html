CKfablab is an adaptive design lab at Yuan Ze University, Taiwan, led by Prof. Chor-Kheng Lim — we build interactive systems, generative works and fabrication experiments that respond to people, objects and environments. CKfablab 是元智大學林楚卿教授帶領的適應性設計實驗室—我們打造能回應人、物件與環境的互動系統、生成創作與數位製造實驗。
